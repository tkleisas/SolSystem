Interpretation
--------------
  Error-per-tick trend     0.9645 (late/early) — steady — the difference grows linearly, i.e. a constant rounding bias
  Final difference         1.288E+06 m after a year
  Energy drift  Fix128     1.524E-12
  Energy drift  double     6.277E-15
  Doubling dt moved the difference 0.99x

  VERDICT: PASS
  The Q64.64 solar frame holds a one-year orbit to 1.37E-06 relative
  error, with energy conserved to one part in 10^12. That is six orders of
  magnitude below anything the simulation can act on. Option A is sound: the
  constraint is the WIDTH of the type, which Fix128 provides and Fix64 cannot.

Width variants — the same orbit, the integrator unchanged, storage narrowed to 64 bits
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Same algorithm, step and initial conditions as the Q64.64 control. All variants are
  a long with Int128 intermediates, so they share one speed class; what differs is
  range, resolution, and which constants stop fitting the type. Every timing keeps the
  last of forty passes: the tiered JIT needs about ten long-loop executions to reach
  final code (set SPIKE_TIMING_TRACE=1 to see the cliff), and a first-pass measurement
  bills the JIT to the type rather than the arithmetic.
